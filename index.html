Why it exists

Most people considering an aesthetic treatment can't decide from words alone. In a published clinical survey of 2,378 patients, the visual simulation was the key decision factor for 58% of them. When people see themselves, deciding takes days instead of years.

Source: Crisalix clinical survey, 2017 — 2,378 patients, 449 clinics, 50 countries.

For people

Understand what's possible, what it looks like on you, and what it should cost — before committing to anything.

For physicians

Patients arrive educated, with realistic expectations and a visual starting point for the consultation.

Not a diagnosis

MIRA is a visualization and education tool. Every treatment decision belongs to a licensed physician.

The technology — and how we use AI

MIRA is built on artificial intelligence, and we're transparent about it: every simulation and analysis in the product is AI-generated. Here is what runs under the hood.

AI visual analysis

Vision models map the face, detect treatable indications, and score their severity — the input for every recommendation.

Generative simulation engines

Generative AI image models render the expected outcome directly on the user's photo, per treatment, at photorealistic quality. Each treatment type runs on the engine that performs best for it.

Physician in the loop

A practicing aesthetic physician reviews and rates simulation quality in blind tests. The AI is calibrated against clinical judgment — continuously.

Privacy & safety

Photos are processed securely and used only to produce the user's own analysis and simulation. Results are presented as a visualization aid — never as medical advice or a guaranteed outcome.
